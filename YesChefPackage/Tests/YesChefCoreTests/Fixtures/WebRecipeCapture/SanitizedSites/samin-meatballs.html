Yotam's Turkey & Zucchini Meatballs

A turkey-and-zucchini meatball dinner with a cool sauce.

For the Sour Cream & Sumac Sauce

  • 1 cup sour cream
  • 1 teaspoon ground sumac

For the Turkey and Zucchini Meatballs

  • 1 pound ground turkey
  • 1 zucchini, grated

Make the sour cream sauce

  1. Stir the sour cream and sumac together.

Shape the meatballs

  1. Combine the turkey and zucchini, then form meatballs.

Cook the meatballs

  1. Cook the meatballs until browned and cooked through.
